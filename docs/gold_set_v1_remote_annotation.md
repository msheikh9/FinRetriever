# Gold set v1: remote annotation packages (withheld)

This document is part of the record of the gold-set v1 human-annotation pilot. The full record names the
volunteer contributors and is **withheld from the public repository** to protect their privacy. It is kept
unchanged, with its integrity hashes, in the project's private records.

**Summary of the pilot:**
- 100 human-written financial research questions were prepared.
- The human relevance annotation was closed as an incomplete pilot. 45 human judgments, all for one question,
  were officially imported.
- The complete human relevance benchmark and the independent annotator-agreement study were not completed.
- No independent human-relevance improvement claim is made for any FinRetriever system.

See the README's limitations section and [research_readiness.md](research_readiness.md).
